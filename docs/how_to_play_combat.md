# How to play a fight

*Player-facing: the words the tutorials and the help card are built from. Written 2026-09-24. Plain English,
second person, no rules jargon the game doesn't show you. The design record behind it is `combat_ux.md`.*

## The short version

1. On your turn you have **two actions**, **one bonus action**, a **reaction**, and your **movement**.
2. Click a lit square to **move**. Press **1–9** (or click a button) to **act**. Click a target to **do it**.
3. **Right-click** or **Esc** if you change your mind — nothing is spent until you confirm.
4. Throw your dice with **Space** when the tray is waiting. Press **Space** again, or **End Turn**, when you're done.

## Your turn

When your turn comes, the squares you can reach light up on the map. Hover one to see the way you'd walk
and how many squares it costs; rough ground costs twice as much. Click to go there. You can move, act, then
move again with what's left.

The **action bar** along the bottom holds everything you can do right now: your weapons, your spells, Dash,
Disengage, Hide, a potion, your class's own tricks. The dots under it are what you have left — **two
actions**, **one bonus action**, **one reaction**. Some classes get a third action later on.

If a button is **greyed out**, hover it: it tells you why ("you've used your bonus action", "nothing in range").

## Attacking and casting

Pick a weapon or a spell and the things you can aim it at are ringed on the map. Hover a target to see:

- your **chance to hit** (or, for a spell they resist, their **chance to fail** the saving throw),
- the **damage** dice,
- and the **damage you can expect** on average — the easy way to compare two choices.

Click the target and your attack dice are thrown on the tray. The number on the felt is the number you rolled.

Some spells cover an area. A **fireball** follows your pointer — everything inside the ring gets caught,
friend or foe, and the preview counts them. A **cone** or a **line** points away from you: turn it with the
**scroll wheel** or **Q** and **E**. If it would catch nobody, the preview says so.

Some spells ask a question first — which damage type, which effect — or let you cast them at a **higher
level** for more power. You'll see a small menu on the bar before you aim.

## Reactions

A reaction happens on someone else's turn: an **opportunity attack** when an enemy walks out of your reach, a
**Shield** when something is about to hit you. Out of the box the game takes a reaction when it clearly helps
you, and never spends a smite you didn't ask for.

You can change that for each reaction in **Settings → Reactions**: *Auto*, *Always*, *Never*, or **Ask**. With
**Ask**, the fight pauses and asks you — for example:

> **Cast Shield?** AC 14 → 19 turns the hit.

There's no timer. Take as long as you like.

## When it's the enemies' turn

The camera follows each enemy, and the log in the corner says what they did. The Game Master rolls behind the
screen — you'll hear it. If you'd rather not watch, press **Space** to skip to the end of their turns, or set the
speed in **Settings**.

## Falling down, and running away

At 0 hit points you fall. At the start of each of your turns you throw **one d20**: **10 or more** and you're
back up; less and it's over — the game takes you back to just before the fight so you can try again.

If a fight is going badly, walk to the edge of the map where the marker is and choose **Flee**. The story
remembers that you ran.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| 1–9 | use a button on the action bar |
| Space | throw the dice · end your turn · skip the enemies' turns |
| Esc, right-click | take back what you picked; with nothing picked, the pause menu |
| Q, E, scroll | turn a cone or line; otherwise turn the table |
| Enter | confirm |
| Tab, Shift+Tab | move between buttons and targets without a mouse |
| L | the fight's log |
| - and = | zoom |

Every key can be changed in **Settings → Access**. With **Read aloud** on, the game says what's focused, why a
button is greyed, and each line of the log.

## Settings that change how fights feel

- **Enemy turn speed** — slow, normal, fast, or instant.
- **Skip throwing the dice** — your rolls happen instantly instead of on the tray.
- **Camera follows enemies** — on or off.
- **Reactions** — Auto, Always, Ask or Never, one by one.
